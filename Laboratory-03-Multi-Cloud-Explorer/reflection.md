# Mission Reflection

### 1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?

Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP), the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **AWS**. I was impressed by the wide range of services that AWS provides for different types of projects and business needs. While exploring AWS, I learned that it offers services for computing, storage, networking, identity management, and many other cloud requirements. It also helped me understand why many organizations use cloud platforms for their applications and systems.

### 2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because all three provide important cloud services such as computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and virtual machines. They also have global infrastructure that allows organizations to run their applications and services through the cloud. Although their services are similar, each platform has different strengths and features.

### 3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements are very important when choosing a cloud platform. A startup may prioritize affordable and scalable services, while a university that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure. An AI research company may need strong AI and machine learning services, while a global e-commerce company may prioritize reliability, high availability, and automatic scaling.

### 4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that I should understand the client's needs before recommending a cloud provider. I learned that there is no single cloud platform that is always the best for every organization. The best choice depends on the client's requirements, budget, existing technologies, and business goals.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio became more organized and complete after this mission. This mission also helped me improve my understanding of AWS, Azure, and GCP.
